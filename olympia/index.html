How Olympia Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? That number says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and is also showing higher bills, uneven temperatures, or frequent repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement in peak season.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet the same part, neglected for a season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.
